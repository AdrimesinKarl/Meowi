03 / A LETTER FOR YOU
Dear Manang,

Happy Teacher's Day to my wonderful sister and one of the people I look up to the most.

Before you became a teacher, you were already my sister who sacrificed so much to provide for me and our family. I grew up seeing how hard you worked and how you always put our family before yourself.

You have made many sacrifices to make sure that we were okay, even when it meant putting your own needs aside.

After all these years of being a teacher, I have also seen how much you care about your students. You have always been someone who puts others before yourself.

We may not be the most affectionate siblings, and I do not always say how I feel, but I want you to know that I appreciate you more than I usually show.

I am proud of you not only because you are a teacher, but because of the kind of sister you have always been. I will always be grateful for the sacrifices you have made for me and for our family.

Thank you for everything you have done for me. I may not say it often, but I see your efforts and I truly appreciate them.

Happy Teacher's Day, Manang. I am proud to have you as my sister.

With love,
Carlo
